# RPT-02: Salon dashboard charts

Status: in progress

## Scope

One evidence dashboard on `/dashboard/reports` with four charts: Appointment Outcome, Sales Trend, Revenue by Service, Employee Commission/Productivity. Date, location, employee, and service filters apply to all four. Each chart has a table alternative in Salon facts. Inventory and loyalty stay 0.

## Out of scope

Four-format export expansion (EXP-01), a second reporting definition, fake inventory/loyalty modules.
